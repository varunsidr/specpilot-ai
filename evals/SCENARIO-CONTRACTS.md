# Deterministic quantity scenario contracts

Implemented 2026-10-11. A requirement can opt into backend quantity checks by declaring `scenarioContracts`. Contracts are supplied by the caller, not inferred from natural language or generated by the planner. Each contract belongs to one numbered acceptance criterion:

```json
{
  "scenarioContracts": [
    { "kind": "quantity_step", "criterion": 1, "direction": "increase", "step": 5, "minimum": 1 },
    { "kind": "quantity_step", "criterion": 2, "direction": "decrease", "step": 5, "minimum": 1 }
  ]
}
```

The caller must ensure the contract agrees with the criterion and intended business rules. Validation requires unique, existing criterion numbers, a supported direction, positive safe integers, and `minimum + 3 * step <= 1000000`. Unsupported fields/kinds are rejected. Requirements without contracts retain ordinary prose scenarios. Contracts apply to drafted gaps; an implemented or unknown assessment does not enter this checker.

## Supported meaning

For increase, an unavailable product, known zero stock, or quantity at/above known stock disables the control and leaves quantity unchanged. Otherwise the operation adds the declared step and clamps to positive known stock; unknown stock (`null`) allows increasing when the product is otherwise available. Partial steps remain enabled.

For decrease, subtract the step and clamp to the minimum. Stock and product unavailability do not govern decreasing. The proposal may disable at the minimum (`at_minimum`) or keep the control enabled with a clamped result (`never`). Both policies are supported, but the action and enabled expectation must agree with the declared guard.

These rules are deliberately limited to the documented quantity domain. They do not decide shipping, pricing, refunds or other missing business policy.

## Draft and correction flow

`quantity-scenarios.ts` creates a fixed set of synthetic starting states, owned by the backend. Increase covers ordinary, partial (when step > 1), limit, zero stock, unknown stock and unavailable product. Decrease covers ordinary, partial, minimum, unknown stock, minimum with unknown stock and unavailable product. Both directions therefore have six cases for step five.

For contracted criteria, the draft returns `testScenarios=[]`, `quantityRule={operation,bound,guard,step,minimum}`, and one `quantityChecks` row per case: `{caseId,action,expectedEnabled,expectedQuantity}`. Case IDs/count/uniqueness and field types are structurally validated. The model cannot alter starting states, omit a boundary, or supply code to execute. Incorrect numeric expectations, including zero, reach deterministic correction rather than being silently normalized.

The backend independently compares declared operation, bounds, step and guard against the caller contract. It calculates enabled state and result for every case, rejects disabled clicks and assertions that skip a required action, and supplies canonical scenario text for the public plan and model review. It also adds the declared rule to the public implementation steps. Public plans retain schema version 2 and string scenarios.

Failures enter the existing single correction loop before model review. A corrected draft is checked again and then reviewed normally. A second deterministic failure rejects it; a subsequent model-review failure also rejects it because the correction budget is shared. `planningMetrics.scenarioAttempts` retains both structured drafts, calculated states and findings. Hardware guards remain in force for live evaluations.

## Evidence and limits

The deterministic tests include the explicit source-reviewed numeric/guard examples in [QUANTITY-REVIEW.md](QUANTITY-REVIEW.md), wrong formulas and partial-step guards, disabled actions, missing/duplicate cases, other steps/minima, and acceptance/rejection through the bounded workflow. A passing check proves agreement of the structured rule and synthetic expectations with the declared contract. It does not prove that prose, a future patch, a UI handler, or a real test fixture implements that rule. Source/evidence review remains necessary; external application and Playwright checks have not run.

The development `quantity-step-buttons` case now declares both contracts. Its gold outcome and source fingerprints remain unchanged, but its requirement/prompt contract changed, so earlier planner scores are historical rather than an isolated accuracy comparison. Reserved validation inputs remain unchanged.

A GPU-verified end-to-end 8B run on 2026-10-11 reached two drafts and rejected both for disabled clicks and incorrect expectations. No model review or accepted plan followed. This exercises the deterministic rejection path in a live model run; generating a correct complete plan remains an open gate. See [README.md](README.md) for the report and measurements.
